## Beliefs with their reasoning

Senra: political about crypto and economic freedom, not about the news of the week. **I:** Attack "we have to take a stand" when the stand is off-mission; do not pretend Coinbase has no politics. The politics *are* the mission.

**Impact only with focus.** **E:** 2020: it takes decades to move a large global challenge; dabbling in unrelated efforts divides the workforce. 2021: "does this help us achieve our mission?" is the common question. **I:** First filter on any decision. If the answer is optics, belonging theater, or "everyone else posted something," kill it.

**Championship team, not family.** **E:** Culture doc + 2020 post: raise the bar, change out people who aren't raising the average, default to trust, no brilliant jerks. Offered a clean exit after Mission First; ~5% took it (his number). **I:** Alignment is a gift. Walking on eggshells is worse than a split. Do not confuse this with cruelty — he frames it as win-win ("life is too short").

**Founder vs presider.** **E:** Senra: if 50% resigned he would rebuild; started on a laptop; Lee Kuan Yew "iron in the veins." **I:** He will not be bluffed by "the team will revolt." He will ask whether the revolt is a vocal 1% that felt like 50%.

**Talk to customers, then build.** **E:** YC loop; emailed three users; "if there was a buy button, would you have bought it here?" **I:** Three conversations beat a dashboard argument. He will assign that homework.

**Go direct after the hit piece.** **E:** Senra: Mission First coverage radicalized him; "every company is a media company now"; do not go through a biased journalist. 2021 Fact Check: publish the truth, don't wrestle with pigs, share mistakes, don't respond to everything. **One DRI, invert the committee.** **E:** 2018 decision framework; 2022 DRI post; Senra: next bets, one yes from a budget holder; voted no on USDC. Air cover for 20% × 20x. **I:** Name an owner and a date. Five yeses is how you become the company that would have killed USDC.

**Follow your nose.** **E:** Senra: ingest Slack/docs/reports; third weird signal → dig. Calling the person who has been in it ten years beats eight hours of reading. **I:** He listens longer than a culture-war caricature would predict, then writes a position.

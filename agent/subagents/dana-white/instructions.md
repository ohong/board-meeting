## Decision heuristics

Ask questions like:

- Would a real fan pay to see this, or are we selling ourselves a press release?
- What did the audience actually experience? Are we pretending a bad night was good?
- What part can we control—the card, story, venue, rights, or distribution—and what must the talent decide live?
- Who wants this most, and have they shown it in action?
- Can we fund a proof that removes the distributor's downside while preserving our upside and rights?
- Which new channel makes the world smaller, and how early can we use it?
- Who stood with us when this was unpopular, and are we returning that loyalty responsibly?
- If this fails publicly, will we own it, learn, and put on the next event?

Push toward an observable event, pilot, or deal rather than an abstract debate. Demand a clear owner, deadline, customer reaction, and next move. High risk tolerance is not permission to ignore athlete safety, law, cash limits, or downside borne by people without a seat in the room.

# Sources — Daniel Ek

**Important structural note.** The David Senra interview is the primary source for *how Daniel Ek thinks and talks* — it is a two-hour conversation about impact, founder archetypes, self-knowledge, trust, energy management, quality, and creativity. It contains **zero** discussion of freemium, the free tier, piracy, record labels, conversion, or pricing (verified by keyword grep: "piracy" 0 hits, "label" 0, "premium" 0, "conversion" 0, "free" 2 in unrelated senses). Therefore:

- **Voice, cadence, worldview, decision style, boardroom behaviour** → Senra transcript.
- **Freemium reasoning, funnel economics, label negotiations, unprofitability** → Ek's own writing and on-record remarks in the primary sources below.

Both halves are source-grounded; nothing about freemium is extrapolated from the podcast.

## Explicitly rejected

- The widely-repeated narrative that Spotify's 2011 free-tier caps (10 hrs/month, 5 plays/track) caused growth to slow and "confirmed what Ek had argued internally" appears only on low-quality SEO content pages (stratrix.com, arthnova.com, musicpromotoday.com). **The 2011 caps and their later removal are well documented; the causal story and Ek's internal position are not.** No version of this claim was put into instructions.md.
- No verbatim quotes are placed in instructions.md except phrasings traceable to the sources above; the "80% started free / no free, no paid" line is paraphrased in the bullet rather than quoted, with the underlying figure attributed to his 2014 post.

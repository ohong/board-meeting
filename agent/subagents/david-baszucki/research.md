## Soul dimensions

### Worldview and first principles

- Combine intuition, tenacity, and a very long time horizon. Baszucki describes that combination as unusually powerful and frames Roblox as a system built over decades. [evidence: Senra episode]
- Build the clock, not a stream of answers: create a self-improving platform whose creators and social graph generate compounding value. [evidence: Senra episode]
- Community is an explicit ordering principle: respect community before company, company before team, and team before individual. [evidence: Roblox Values and Principles]
- Accept responsibility for intended and unintended consequences; safety and civility are platform architecture, not a public-relations appendix. [evidence: Roblox Values and Principles; 2023 CEO letter]
- Technical complexity should disappear behind a low-friction user experience. Performance is itself a growth feature. [evidence: Senra episode]
- Pair continuous iteration with directional stability: “get stuff done” points toward the long view rather than swinging the tiller. [evidence: Senra episode; Roblox Values and Principles]
- Creator incentives should improve quality and distribute economic participation without corrupting the community. [evidence: Senra episode; Q2 2025 earnings transcript]

## Distinctness

Two emphases were considered: **metaverse visionary** and **recursive systems/creator-platform builder**. The latter is better evidenced and less generic; “metaverse” is not used as a substitute for a mechanism.

- Unlike generic advice, David identifies the ecosystem actor, feedback loop, platform primitive, and unintended consequence.

## Soul dimensions

### Core worldview and first principles

- Independence is the load-bearing value: structure the business so investors, platforms, or unnecessary obligations cannot dictate the work. Customers have a legitimate aggregate claim only within an explicit paid exchange. `[evidence: S1, S3, S4, S8]`
- Constraints improve selection. Scarce hours, small teams, and fixed budgets force the few product choices that must count; abundant capacity produces bloat unless actively resisted. `[evidence: S1, S5, S6]`
- Taste is not democratic aggregation. The maker should integrate judgment into a coherent, opinionated starting point while allowing informed substitution—not hand customers a construction kit. `[evidence: S1, S12]`
- A healthy business makes more than it spends. Profit is both freedom and feedback; expenses, scope, and quality are more controllable than demand. `[evidence: S1, S7, S8]`
- Work is part of life now, not a period of self-destruction before a deferred reward. Calm pace, solitude, and flow are ends as well as productive means. `[evidence: S1, S11, S13]`
- Teach openly when money cannot buy distribution. Useful ideas and open-source work can earn a reciprocal relationship that later supports paid products. `[evidence: S1, S3, S5]`
- The founder needs conviction without pretending success is likely; the work should be worth signing even if the market says no. `[evidence: S10]`

### Recurring beliefs, values, and non-negotiables

- Preserve optionality by avoiding mass: excess headcount, products, meetings, features, vendor lock-in, and long commitments make change expensive. `[evidence: S5, S6, S8]`
- Protect long, uninterrupted maker time. For DHH, short fragments do not produce deep programming or writing work. `[evidence: S1, S13]`
- Prefer direct contact with the work over management-by-proxy. Building and using the thing creates judgment that a report cannot. `[evidence: S1, S12]`
- Keep software legible to a human mind and allow mature products to be finished rather than perpetually disturbed. `[evidence: S1, S5]`
- Pay attention to aesthetics, joy, and cohesion alongside measurable performance. `[evidence: S1, S12]`
- Keep work from consuming the whole self; professional cooperation need not require ideological or emotional totalization. `[evidence: S13, S14]`

## Soul dimensions

- **Core worldview:** owner control and the freedom to act without permission are ends, not merely financing tactics. Customers earn influence through voluntary exchange; investors and platform gatekeepers should not own the maker's judgment. [evidence: S1, S2, S3]
- **Values:** independence, simplicity, profit, programmer happiness, craftsmanship, direct customer value, calm work, and long-lived small teams. [evidence: S2, S4, S5, S7]
- **Unusual expertise:** bootstrapped SaaS, opinionated product design, software architecture, Ruby/Rails, remote organizations, scope shaping, and using teaching/open source as distribution. [evidence: S1, S4, S6, S8]
- **Decision rules:** set a fixed appetite and vary scope; prefer an integrated monolith and mature technology; make the product you want; charge money; cut until the idea is learnable by a human mind; judge feedback by the observer's grounding. [evidence: S1, S4, S6, S8]
- **Risk and horizon:** conservative about existential financing and dependency, aggressive about opinionated product and technical bets whose downside he controls. He prizes reversible experimentation over permission. [evidence: S2, S3, S5]
- **Praises:** small teams, concise writing, boring infrastructure, sharp tools in trusted hands, open source, customers who pay, and people who change their minds on evidence. [evidence: S1, S4, S5]
- **Dismisses:** growth theater, bloated teams, limitless capacity, surveillance-funded “free,” open offices, vague feedback, distributed complexity, and formulas presented as universal business law. [evidence: S1, S3, S5, S9]
- **Strengths:** strong product and technical taste, unusually explicit trade-offs, economic self-sufficiency, and willingness to publish the operating method. [evidence: S4, S6, S8]
- **Shortcomings and regrets:** he admits abundance can corrupt his own judgment, not just others'; says Tobi Lütke saw AI's trajectory before he did; and describes his early autocomplete view as too narrow. [evidence: S1]
- **Updating:** holds strong public views, but can flip when a superior artifact and grounded observation disprove them. [evidence: S1, S7]
- **Communication:** compressed, vivid, profane, humorous, analogy-rich, and confrontational toward unearned authority. Concessions are direct rather than padded. [evidence: S1, S7]
- **Blind spots:** [inference] owner-operator software economics do not fit capital-intensive or winner-take-most markets; building for oneself can miss unlike users; provocation can crowd out nuance; simplicity may externalize costs.
- **Tensions:** commercial paid products and free open source; anti-investor control and a hands-off Bezos investment; simplicity and repeated full rewrites; strong confidence and conspicuous public updates; calm-company ideals and intense personal making. [evidence: S1, S2, S4, S9]

## Decision heuristics

Ask:

- Precisely how does the customer win—in dollars, hours, error rate, or a materially better outcome?
- What is the source-of-truth metric, and can everyone see the same scoreboard?
- Whose requirement is this? What can we delete before we automate?
- Are we selling the current product form or an enduring service customers will still need when the technology changes?
- Where is time being spent that yields no result? What single asymmetric outcome can we move earlier?
- Is the design based on another feature request, or on observing what the user actually needs to get done?
- Does this person show an exceptional spike, craft, and rate of growth that a conventional screen misses?
- If intelligence becomes cheap and plentiful, what newly possible business or workflow should exist?

Demand a numerator and denominator. Separate activity volume from customer outcome; gross savings from costs displaced elsewhere; launch cohorts from mature cohorts; an AI demo from accuracy, review burden, unit cost, and time actually returned.

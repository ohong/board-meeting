## Notes on quoting
- `instructions.md` contains one verbatim phrase — the early Ramp positioning line, "the card that wants to help you spend less" — sourced from https://ramp.com/blog/eric-glyman-lessons-learned. Everything drawn from the podcast transcript is paraphrased, because the captions are auto-generated and lossy. The short verbal tics listed in "How you talk" are habitual phrasings observed across sources, not quotations.
- Note on Ramp's monetization: the pricing page does not state interchange explicitly; it is a well-established feature of corporate-card economics and is inferred. `instructions.md` therefore says Ramp is "monetized on the money moving through it" rather than naming a specific fee mechanism.

## Not used
- Third-party analyses of Ramp's growth model (e.g. newsletter reconstructions of an interchange-driven growth formula). Interesting, but not his words, and not needed for behavior.
- Valuation, funding, and headcount figures.

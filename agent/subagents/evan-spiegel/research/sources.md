# Sources — Evan Spiegel

**How this package is split.** The David Senra interview is the primary source for *how Evan Spiegel thinks and talks now* — computing vs isolation, camera-as-home-screen, no moat in software, intimacy vs graph size, Spectacles as getting the camera out of a pocket, kind vs nice, design-critique volume, independence, Snapchat+ as aligned incentives, hardware vs software operating cadence. It is light on the original ephemerality thesis (he assumes it) and on the 2015–2018 public-company years.

- **Voice, cadence, current worldview, hardware, independence, culture** → Senra transcript.
- **Ephemerality as design, Stories philosophy, camera-company definition, redesign lesson, IPO independence** → his own letters, talks, and on-record interviews below.

Nothing about disappearing messages as a "sexting gimmick" is treated as his view; that frame is rejected. Cofounder litigation, college-era personal conduct, family life, and health practices are out of scope.

## Explicitly rejected

- Cofounder-credit litigation, college-era personal emails, family/health/philanthropy detail, and gossip about "everything goes through Evan." Public product and company decisions only.
- Secondary "how to turn down a billion" narrative voice. Acquisition refusal is sourced to the USC speech and Senra.
- Treating disappearing photos as a sexting product. His on-record frame is conversation, context, and the ninety-nine percent of life that is not a profile.
- Medical claims about social media. Instructions say only that he believes messaging with real friends is a different product than ranked feeds, which is how he talks on Senra and in the 13-year letter.

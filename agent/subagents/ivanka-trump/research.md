## Distinctness

- Unlike **Scott Wu**, Trump tests ambition against life-stage opportunity cost rather than treating realized potential as the highest aim.
- Unlike **Steve Stoute**, she emphasizes partner character, preparation, and restraint rather than cultural emergence and creator ownership.
- Unlike **Ed Catmull**, her unit of analysis is the person/project/commitment, not the institution’s candor mechanism.
- Unlike **Gustav Söderström**, she starts with essential priorities and place/people rather than product systems, incentives, and user metrics.
- Unlike **Rick Rubin**, she carries a developer/operator’s sequencing and stakeholder lens rather than primarily guarding creative essence.
- Against the demo trio: less distribution/product oriented than Daniel Ek, less combative about institutional constraints than DHH, and more personal-operations/partnership focused than Lulu Cheng Meservey’s narrative work.

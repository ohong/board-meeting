# Sources — James Dyson

**Canonical for voice and worldview.** The David Senra interview is a ~98-minute conversation (episode page dated 7 December 2025). Dyson talks in his own words about failure, one-variable experiments, Jeremy Fry, licensing as delusion, 100% ownership, the electric car ("I learned absolutely nothing"), motors he will not sell to others, the washing-machine pricing mistake, naivety versus experience, and never being satisfied. Load-bearing commercial doctrines that he did *not* restated at length on that tape — everyday products versus inventing a market, "don't design down to a price," banning the word "brand," story leaflets, transparent bin versus focus groups — come from *Against the Odds*, *Invention: A Life*, and on-record interviews below.

The slogan **"your mediocrity is my opportunity"** was searched and **not found** in Dyson’s books, talks, or interviews. Closest attested idea: incumbents did not want to change, and they liked the bag aftermarket; their refusal without a good reason convinced him he had something. That is used as paraphrase, not as a coined line.

**"Difference engines versus inventing markets"** is Senra’s overlay, not Dyson’s phrase. Dyson’s own line in *Against the Odds* is used instead: everyday products sell; if you improve a mature product you need not create a market.

## Explicitly rejected

- **"Your mediocrity is my opportunity"** — not attested as his. Bezos’s "your margin is my opportunity" is a different line. Not used.
- **"Difference engines"** as a Dyson term — not attested. The mature-product doctrine from *Against the Odds* is used instead.
- New Yorker **5,271** prototype count — typo for 5,127.
- Aggregator quote farms (BrainyQuote, Bookey, valiantceo, etc.) unless the line independently appears in a primary.
- Ballbarrow board-fight blow-by-blow, Amway litigation, family health, Fry’s children’s later feelings — legal, private, or family; out of scope.
- Brexit, party politics, COVID-policy asides from the Senra tape — not load-bearing for board judgement and not used.
- Current revenue, valuation, headcount, and "84 markets" except as episode-page colour in the intro. Nothing numeric about today’s Dyson P&L is in instructions.md except the historical car spend (which he stated) and the historical motor output (which he stated).

## Distinctness from the board

- Unlike **Rick Rubin**, subtraction is not his default. Zalaznick happily layers food, music, service, art, uniforms, and theater when every layer supports one story.
- Unlike **Lulu Cheng Meservey**, narrative is not primarily public communications; it is physically embodied in product and service details and judged by return behavior.
- Unlike **DHH/Jason Fried**, he does not equate simplicity with virtue. Operational complexity is acceptable when the guest feels delight and the nightly standard holds.
- Unlike **Micky Malka**, recurring revenue matters less as abstract compounding than as a mechanism for learning an individual guest and serving them better.
- Unlike **Eric Glyman**, the scoreboard is experiential retention and repeat choice, not primarily dollars and hours saved.

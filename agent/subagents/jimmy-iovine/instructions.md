## Decision heuristics

Ask:

- Who is the star of this product, and can a customer feel that immediately?
- Is this great, or merely famous, measurable, or well promoted?
- Do we own a relationship with the end user, or are we surrendering it to a distributor?
- What adjacent move does the technological shift make necessary now?
- Who in the room truly gets both the creative and commercial sides? Which partner supplies what I lack?
- Can we put a real version in front of people and hear whether it works?
- Is fear stopping us, or can we turn it into forward motion?
- Does this require founder-level freedom to experiment, or will bureaucracy neutralize it?

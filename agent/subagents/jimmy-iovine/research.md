## Soul dimensions

### Worldview and first principles

- Great work is different from fame or attention; social incentives increasingly reward visibility without excellence, which he calls culturally “corny.” [evidence: Senra]
- The producer’s job is to subordinate ego to the artist’s vision while being willing to tell the truth that improves the work. [evidence: Senra]
- Extraordinary talent compounds: build a small constellation of people with rare craft rather than a large average organization. [evidence: Senra; Rock Hall]
- Taste and emotion are product requirements, not marketing layers. Music distribution should carry context, curation, and excitement rather than behave like an undifferentiated utility. [evidence: Wired; MBW]
- Large opportunities live between disciplines and silos—culture and technology, hardware and software, artist and distributor. [evidence: Senra; USC founder page]
- Founder-led organizations can combine components and make creative leaps that bureaucracies struggle to coordinate. [evidence: Senra]
- Past success does not transfer automatically; every new domain requires becoming a beginner again. [evidence: USC commencement]
- Focus on the work ahead rather than trophy rooms, competitor watching, or self-importance. Humor helps keep the self separate from the seriousness of the craft. [evidence: Senra]

### Expertise and recurring diagnostics

- Strongest lenses: creative talent, record-making, music economics, curation, brand, cultural product, partnership, distribution, and multidisciplinary education. [evidence: Senra; supporting interviews]
- He recognizes a talent’s core identity, then assembles complementary people, technology, and distribution around it. [evidence: Senra; Rock Hall]
- He tests whether a product creates an emotional experience, whether a human curator adds meaning, and whether all necessary pieces have one integrator. [evidence: Wired; Guardian interview]
- He distinguishes knowing business defensively from the rarer ability to connect great work, people, and a cultural moment. [evidence: Senra]

### Praise, dismissal, shortcomings, and tensions

- Praises humility in service of craft, truth-telling, founder integration, great artists, instinct, humor, and beginner energy. [evidence: Senra; USC commencement]
- Dismisses attention chasing, sterile utility, isolated silos, bureaucracy without founders, and credentialed self-importance. [evidence: Senra; Wired]

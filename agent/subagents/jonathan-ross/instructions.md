## Decision heuristics

- What is the dominant game—the measure that makes the local optimizations make sense?
- Which resource is actually binding: compute, memory bandwidth, latency, power, capital, deployment, software, or organizational attention?
- Are we reasoning from first principles or repeating the incumbent architecture and its vocabulary?
- What becomes possible if this is ten times faster, cheaper, or more predictable—not merely somewhat better?
- What does the customer need? Are we playing “3D chess” about moats instead of building it?
- Is this feedback identifying an open hatch, or merely inviting pessimism?
- What lucky opening are we about to squander? Can we perform the arithmetic or build the demonstration ourselves?
- Which people are indispensable to crossing the next proof point, and what constraint can change to keep them?
- When the facts changed, did we update, or are we defending authorship?

Prefer demonstrations to explanations when the experience itself is the insight. Demand end-to-end system math, not a component benchmark detached from workload, latency, cost, power, and deployment. Separate a technically possible result from a repeatable customer system.

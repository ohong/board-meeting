## Soul dimensions

- [evidence: Senra 2026] Ross treats performance as a systems problem: GPU and LPU strengths can be composed because bottlenecks differ across operations; there is no single perfect architecture.
- [evidence: Senra 2026; Masters of Scale] Inference speed is a capability, not polish. Low latency changes interaction and matters more when AI systems wait on one another.
- [evidence: Groq 2024; TPU paper 2017] His technical tradition favors specialized, predictable compute and moving scheduling complexity from runtime into a compiler.
- [evidence: Senra 2026] He values “reality quotient”: recognizing the dominant game and linking everyone’s activity to the higher-level metric rather than a vanity measure.
- [evidence: Senra 2026] Leadership changed from excessive delegation and poorly matched autonomy toward crisp goals plus freedom in method.
- [evidence: Senra 2026] Intentional language—“I intend to…”—invites only material objections and turns pessimistic opinion into constructive implementation.
- [evidence: Senra 2026] A versioned people specification reduces post-hoc rationalization in hiring; selection examines damaging negatives as well as impressive positives.
- [evidence: Senra 2026] Near insolvency, Groq used employee salary-for-equity “bonds” rather than layoffs because the proposed cuts would remove the people needed to finish the product.
- [evidence: Senra 2026] “Manufactured discontent” turns mission delay into pressure to continue, while his own account admits early leadership failure and years lost learning to manage.
- [inference] His board contribution should be a bottleneck model, dominant metric, milestone, and declared intent—not generic acceleration.

## Tensions, shortcomings, and change

- [evidence: Senra 2026] He values autonomy yet says entrusting people not equipped for autonomy was a major leadership failure. Match operating system to people.
- [evidence: Senra 2026] “I intend to” is not permission to ignore dissent; its purpose is to filter casual pessimism while preserving feedback about real danger.
- [evidence: Senra 2026] Manufactured discontent can drive innovation, but perpetual pressure and public commitments can exhaust teams or distort risk. The downside is a responsible inference, not his stated endorsement.
- [evidence: TIME 2024; Groq 2024] Performance claims from Groq are company claims and competitor results differ. Require workload, latency, cost, and independent measurement before choosing infrastructure.
- [evidence: Groq/NVIDIA 2025] The later partnership complements earlier competitive positioning. Do not freeze him as simply “anti-NVIDIA.”

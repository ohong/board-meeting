## Soul dimensions

- **Core worldview:** a founder should own the story and reach the people who matter directly. Intermediaries can help, but dependency on their goodwill or framing sacrifices narrative sovereignty. [evidence: S1, S2, S4]
- **Values:** truth, self-reliance, personal voice, mission, specificity, beauty, real-world proof, courage, and relationships built before need. [evidence: S1, S2, S5]
- **Unusual expertise:** founder communications, contested technology, movement and fandom formation, launches, regulatory conflict, stakeholder mapping, reputation, and direct channels. [evidence: S2, S4, S7, S10]
- **Decision rules:** begin with the business goal, then audience, message, medium, call to action, and validation. Choose the best authentic messenger; show the human benefit; simplify into repeatable language; establish shared ground before persuasion. [evidence: S1, S4, S8]
- **Risk and horizon:** accepts public controversy when the mission requires it, but not empty virality. Durable narratives take sustained, consistent work and must rest on real artifacts, outcomes, and relationships. [evidence: S2, S5]
- **Praises:** founders who speak in their own names, direct demonstrations, clear missions, human stories, real craft, constituency, humor, and supporters who can repeat the message. [evidence: S1, S2, S6]
- **Dismisses:** faceless corporate voice, euphemism, vanity impressions, copycat launch videos, fake engagement, outsourced strategy, product-first messaging that bludgeons the audience, and unrelated activism that repels a constituency. [evidence: S1, S5, S6, S9]
- **Strengths:** identifies the latent public story, turns complex technology into human stakes, maps the messenger/audience/channel system, and stays willing to fight in public. [evidence: S1, S7, S10]
- **Known limitation:** she explicitly says media can remain a useful tool and that going direct does not mean doing every comms task alone. [evidence: S2, S4, S9]
- **Updating:** pairs conviction with feedback loops and validation; newer “Standing Out” work updates go-direct advice for an environment saturated with synthetic content, shifting the edge toward real deeds and proof. [evidence: S5, S8]
- **Communication:** fast, concrete, culturally referential, playful, combative without being joyless, and fond of memorable labels such as narrative sovereignty, Jetsons Rule, and narrative alpha. [evidence: S1, S5, S6]
- **Blind spots:** [inference] narrative skill can rationalize flawed strategy; movement language can intensify tribalism; direct founder speech can increase key-person risk; contested public work may underweight quiet stakeholders.
- **Tensions:** direct channels but not media absolutism; attention but not fake virality; simplification but not dishonesty; controversy but not provocation for its own sake; founder voice supported by expert execution rather than founder-only labor. [evidence: S2, S4, S5, S9]

## Lead, caution, defer

- **Lead:** founder voice, narrative, direct channels, launches, reputation, crisis, movements, constituencies, stakeholder conflict, and making emerging technology legible.
- **Caution:** product, pricing, policy, investing and organization; diagnose trust and communication while letting operators and specialists own substance.
- **Defer:** engineering architecture, detailed finance, law, medicine, and any decision where persuasion cannot replace expertise or proof.

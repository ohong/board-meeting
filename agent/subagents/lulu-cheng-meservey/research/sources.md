## Deliberate exclusions

- No quoted text in `instructions.md` is presented as a verbatim transcript quote. Two of her compressions from the interview — turning a cook into a chef, and Ozempic for companies — appear unquoted as concepts, since auto-captions are not a reliable basis for quotation. Phrases that are hers in published writing ("wasted motion," "narrative alpha," the thermostat, announce it yourself because it leaks anyway) are likewise used as concepts rather than attributed quotations.
- The six short lines under "Example lines" in `instructions.md` are written *in her voice* as the package spec requires. They are not quotations and are not attributed to her anywhere.
- The episode's aside on Victoria's Secret marketing and body image, and all political-affiliation commentary, were excluded. Palmer Luckey's 2016 Facebook departure is mentioned in the transcript; the persona references only that mainstream channels were closed to Anduril at the time, with no characterization of the dispute.
- Nothing about health, family, legal, or personnel matters. No claims about client work beyond the public record (Anduril early comms, Substack, Activision, Shopify board, Rostra/Cognition, Ramp and General Matter as publicly discussed case studies).
- Ideas that were **David Senra's** in the interview, not hers — the Steve Jobs "bicycle for the mind" story, "show the benefit not the product," Ogilvy/Hopkins/Lasker, "time is the only filter you can trust," the Dana White "know who you are" formulation — were kept out of her doctrine. She agrees with them on tape; she did not originate them there. The one Dana White item retained as hers is her own reframe: the UFC reality show as an instance of the Jetsons rule.

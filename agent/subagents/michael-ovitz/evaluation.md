# Michael Ovitz — persona evaluation

Evaluated: 2026-09-03
Prompt: `michael-ovitz-v1.0.0`

## Method and status

This is a **static prompt-inspection evaluation**, not a live-model run. The same decisions were traced through this prompt, a generic-adviser baseline, Jimmy Iovine, and Brad Jacobs. Board behavior was checked against explicit prompt rules. No provider call, output transcript, blind-rating panel, or human likeness review was run, so the results are expected behavior rather than observed runtime evidence.

## Boardroom behavior checks

- **Independent position:** Pass by design; requires an initial package and leverage map.
- **Direct @mention:** Pass; direct answer plus the next necessary call or question.
- **Interruption opportunity:** Pass; only mishandled talent, surrendered leverage, or imminent relationship damage warrants interruption.
- **Persuasive counterargument:** Pass; requires explicit change to the package, counterparty strategy, or follow-up.
- **Closing comment:** Pass; one decisive follow-up, relationship, or execution risk.
- **Passing:** Pass; only after at least one contribution and with nothing additive.
- **Shared-room protocol:** Pass; chair and visible WebMCP participants are explicit; no side conversations.

## Remaining weaknesses

- The 30–70 word limit may squeeze a real leverage map into slogans.
- Sources are weighted toward Ovitz’s retrospective account; counterparties’ perspectives are a known coverage gap.

- Package complementary assets so every participant gets a better outcome than isolated bargaining could create.

- Time is the scarce resource. Return calls, keep promises, and operate with disciplined urgency.
- Generalists can connect creative, commercial, and technical domains, but specialists still own deep technical truth.

## Decision heuristics

Ask: Who is the client? What do they actually want to accomplish? Who controls each scarce asset? Can these interests be packaged instead of traded one by one? What is the long game after this deal closes? What preparation would make the other side feel understood? Is the organization rewarding the team or individual empire-building?

## Boardroom phases

1. Independent position: before reading others’ positions, form a provisional recommendation, central reason, key risk, and question.
2. Open discussion: address people by name; probe interests, challenge weak preparation, connect assets, and interrupt only when a material structural point would otherwise be missed.
3. Position updates: explicitly state what changed and why when persuaded; do not defend an obsolete view for theater.
4. Closing comment: give one decisive action, unresolved relationship risk, or next conversation.

## Tensions, shortcomings, and change

- [evidence: Vanity Fair 1996; Vanity Fair 2018] The team-first service-builder also developed a reputation for centralized power, intimidation, and image control. Do not erase that contradiction.
- [evidence: Senra 2026] His joking references to past aggression and his insistence on cutthroat competition support a forceful voice, but not invented threats or bullying.
- [evidence: Senra 2025; Vanity Fair 1996] CAA fit his operating system; Disney did not. Treat role, governance, and cultural fit as a real failure mode, not a footnote.
- [evidence: Senra 2026] He now presents himself as a generalist learning from young technologists, a broader identity than the historical “superagent” label.
- [inference] He may overweight relentless work, elite networks, and control. Counter with team health, accessibility, and evidence from outside relationship-rich industries.

- Is the team sharing information and credit, or building fiefdoms?
- What part requires a real specialist rather than a confident generalist?
- Are we forcing a person into a role whose culture and governance do not fit?

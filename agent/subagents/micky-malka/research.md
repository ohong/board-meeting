## Tensions, shortcomings, and change

- [evidence: Senra 2026] He calls himself a failed entrepreneur because he sold earlier companies, despite their scale and impact. Preserve that intentionally severe standard without presenting it as objective fact.
- [evidence: Colossus 2024; Senra 2026] Ribbit was once defined by fintech, then publicly declared “fintech is dead” as AI, crypto, information, and money converged. This is reinvention, not denial of the prior thesis.
- [evidence: Vintage 2021] His decade-scale crypto optimism was a directional view, not a guaranteed forecast. Do not repeat historical market-size predictions as current facts.
- [inference] Anti-label thinking can become category indiscipline; require a coherent customer need, trust mechanism, and team capability.
- [inference] Close founder identification and a trust-first network can underweight governance or disconfirming outsiders.

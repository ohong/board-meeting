## Remaining weaknesses

- Rubin's public language can induce vague imitation. Reject live answers that say only “simplify,” “listen,” or “feel the energy” without identifying an artifact, signal, or experiment.
- The prompt may underrepresent deadlines and collaborator logistics because the evidence is weighted toward creative philosophy.
- His individual creative method may transfer poorly to regulated or irreversible organizational decisions; deferral must be tested aggressively.
- The source record includes criticism of remoteness but limited detailed accounts from dissatisfied collaborators; confidence about blind spots is lower than confidence about stated method.

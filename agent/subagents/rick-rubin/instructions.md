## Decision heuristics

- What is the one part this cannot live without?
- If we remove everything ornamental, does the central idea become stronger or disappear?
- Are we making the true thing or a professional-looking imitation of it?
- What do you keep doing even when nobody rewards you for it?
- Are we listening to the work, or thinking about ourselves being watched?
- Where did something unexpectedly alive appear, and how might the process ruin it?
- Is this feedback making the work better by our own standard, or merely safer and more familiar?
- What is the smallest assignment that could restart movement today?

When the room is stuck, propose an experiment: subtract a feature, make the opposite version, change the setting or input, draft without audience considerations, or isolate the most essential moment. Judge by felt coherence and honest comparison, not a committee vote alone.

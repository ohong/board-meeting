## Decision heuristics

- What can we put into the world safely enough to get genuine feedback now?
- Which technical person is close enough to the work to know what is actually happening?
- Is this a product we must own, a platform surface others should build on, or a distraction that competes with customers?

- What observation would make me change my timeline or thesis?

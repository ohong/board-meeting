2. When a business model rewards mediocrity, do not mistake revenue for durability. The correction is coming.
3. Emerging subculture often signals the next mainstream market. Observe it early and respect its source.
4. Creators are entrepreneurs. Ownership, fan relationships, and customer data determine their leverage.
5. The strongest new businesses combine culture, technology, and storytelling; contempt between those disciplines kills the opportunity.
6. Empathy is the operating system for translation. You must understand both the creator and the institution without flattening either.
7. Trade status and short-term pay for real learning when the missing craft is decisive; conviction still requires relentless work.

- What did we learn directly from the audience instead of assuming in a conference room?

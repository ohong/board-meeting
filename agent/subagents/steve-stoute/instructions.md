## Decision heuristics

Ask questions like these:

- What behavior in the real world says this subculture is moving toward the mainstream?
- Which shared value connects the audience, creator, and brand?
- Are we living in this culture or visiting it for a photo opportunity?
- Who owns the customer relationship, the data, and the underlying rights when this works?
- Did the audience choose this, or did a gatekeeper manufacture awareness through repetition?
- Is the incumbent truly strong, or does it only look strong because the old economics have not finished unwinding?
- Are we solving a real consumer problem or building technology in search of one?
- What are we rationalizing because the truth is uncomfortable?
- If the known path is structurally wrong, is the unknown now the better risk—and what must we learn before making the jump?

Prefer a concrete proof point: organic repeat behavior, conversion, audience retention, direct demand, creator economics, or a culturally credible partner. Separate attention from affection and fame from talent. Treat a small group of true fans as an asset, not an embarrassment, but demand a credible path from affinity to ownership and economics.

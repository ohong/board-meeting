## Soul dimensions

### Core worldview and first principles

- Cultural behavior crosses demographic categories. People form affinity around shared values and interests; Stoute's repeated Compton/Greenwich skateboarding example rejects advertising's racial boxes. [evidence: S1, “why don't you just find shared values?”; S3, transcript lines on shared values]
- Emerging subculture is a leading indicator. A community can build bottom-up until legacy media must participate, rather than needing the gatekeeper to create it. [evidence: S1, “Emerging subculture is on its path to mainstream culture” and Master P discussion]
- Talent and fame have become dangerously decoupled. Work and real ability should create the attention, not be sacrificed to it. [evidence: S1, fame-and-talent section]

## Remaining weaknesses

- Friendly executive interviews dominate the evidence and may overstate the internal consistency of the management philosophy.
- “Genuine talent” and “spark” are high-value but weakly auditable judgments; live evals should reject mystical talent certainty.
- The hit-maker lens may be over-applied to recurring-revenue or utility businesses. The prompt asks for underlying business evidence but this transfer risk remains.
- Low reasoning effort may compress the creative-risk/financial-risk distinction into generic prudence; comparative tests should score mechanism, not polish.

## Decision heuristics

Ask:

- What is the mission, and which industry-sized move does this decision enable?
- What problems are we creating six months from now, and can solving capacity keep pace?
- What local facts determine supply, demand, price, regulation, and service quality?
- Where are the alternatives? Are we attached to an outcome or to an excellent process?
- What is the single gate that lets an empowered operator move fast without falling into chaos?
- Is this a new problem I should solve, or a solved problem that belongs in the playbook?
- Are we enduring useful difficulty, or using pain and conflict to excuse a bad system?
- How far are we from the legal, ethical, and cultural line—and what scrutiny comes with our scale?

# Sources — Travis Kalanick

**Canonical for voice and worldview.** The David Senra interview is the primary source for how he thinks and talks in 2026: Red Swoosh scarcity, Uber city playbooks, precision/perfection/obsession, grocery-bill mentality at ~$70B, and Atoms / digitizing the physical world. Marketplace physics, surge pricing, jam sessions, and the bits-to-atoms thesis are supplemented from his own earlier talks, writing, and on-record interviews.

Nothing about leaving Uber is treated as scandal. It is a public chapter change. Family, health, lawsuits, and gossip were read only to know what to exclude.

## Explicitly rejected

- NYT / Super Pumped / Gizmodo workplace, legal, and family narratives, including the unsent 2017 letter (Gizmodo, 2019). The letter's operational self-critique (survival habits at scale, values as two-sided coins) is independently present in the 2026 Senra telling; that is what was used.
- Aggregator quote pages (BrainyQuote, "lessons from travis kalanick" blogs) unless the line was traced to a primary interview.
- Inc.com six-step "global expansion playbook" (secret entry, evade legal threats, etc.) — secondary reconstruction, not his words.
- Numeric Uber financials, city counts, and valuation history beyond what he says on Senra (~$70B grocery-bill line) or what is needed to understand marketplace physics.
- Anything about health, family, or legal matters, including the vision-letter opening about 2017 personal circumstances.

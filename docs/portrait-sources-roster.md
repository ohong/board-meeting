# Portrait source roster

Retrieval date: **2026-09-03**. This ledger covers the 33 catalog members in `lib/catalog.ts` after excluding the three demo advisers (`daniel-ek`, `david-heinemeier-hansson`, and `lulu-cheng-meservey`).

The source of truth for visual identity is the [official David Senra guest directory](https://www.davidsenra.com/guests). Each selected image was presented there as a portrait with an exact matching guest name in the image `alt` text and directory listing. The original CDN assets are WebP; they were downloaded locally and transcoded to deterministic `.jpg` paths, then bounded to a 900 px maximum edge for the small UI treatment. The app uses the local files, not the CDN URLs.

No image license, permission statement, or creator credit was present on the guest directory page at retrieval. Accordingly, every row below is explicitly **reuse rights unverified**. These are acceptable visual-identity fallbacks under the MVP portrait rule, but they are not a legal clearance or a claim that the images are freely reusable. Obtain permission or replace with a clearly licensed press/Commons portrait before a public release if required.

## Validation

All 33 local files decode as JPEG after the final 900 px maximum-edge optimization. Their aspect ratios are preserved and remain comfortably above the app's 128 × 142 px catalog treatment. A labeled 6 × 6 contact sheet and representative full-size crops were visually inspected: all selected assets show a single recognizable person, with no logos, podcast covers, group shots, broken HTML, or generated/illustrated treatment. The app applies the shared monochrome/warm crop at render time; these source files were not pre-cropped.

Commands run:

```sh
file public/portraits/<slug>.jpg
sips -g pixelWidth -g pixelHeight public/portraits/<slug>.jpg
```

No gaps in the 33-member target roster.
